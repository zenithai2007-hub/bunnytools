Uploading a photo to a job portal or exam form and getting the dreaded "file too large" error? Most application forms in Pakistan and India require your photo or signature to be under 50KB or 100KB, while a single phone-camera picture is usually 2–5MB. That is 40 to 100 times bigger than allowed. Compressing an image to 50KB sounds technical, but it takes less than a minute with a free online tool.

Why do forms ask for 50KB?

Small files upload fast even on slow mobile connections, cost less storage for the organization, and open instantly for the reviewer. A 50KB photo is still perfectly clear for ID purposes when compressed correctly.

1. Open the BunnyTools Image Compressor — no signup needed, it works right in your browser.

3 tips for the best result

First, use JPG for photos — it compresses far better than PNG. PNG is only better for screenshots or images with text. Second, resize the dimensions first: a photo that is 800 pixels wide is plenty for an online form and compresses much more easily than a 4000-pixel original. Third, always zoom in and check faces and text after compressing — if it looks blurry, raise the quality slightly and try 100KB instead.
