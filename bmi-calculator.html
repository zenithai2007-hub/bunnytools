BMI Calculator — Check Your Body Mass Index

Want a quick health snapshot? This BMI calculator turns your height and weight into a body mass index score plus your category — underweight, normal, overweight, or obese. Check BMI online in seconds, free.

How to use: 1) Enter your height and weight. 2) Hit calculate. 3) See your BMI and category instantly.

Fitness beginners track their starting point, gym-goers monitor progress, and anyone curious gets a ballpark figure fast. Note: BMI is a rough guide, not a medical diagnosis — for health decisions, talk to a professional. No signup, fully private.
