🔲 How to Create a WiFi QR Code Free

Let guests join your WiFi with one scan — no more spelling out long passwords.

Guests at your home — or customers at your shop — keep asking for the WiFi password, and spelling out a 16-character password letter by letter is painful. A WiFi QR code solves this: one scan and the phone joins the network automatically, no typing at all. Here is how to create a WiFi QR code free.

Why use a WiFi QR code?

It is faster, avoids mistakes ("was that a zero or the letter O?"), and you never have to say your password out loud in front of everyone. Cafes, barbershops, and clinics print theirs and stick it on the counter.

Create yours in 4 steps

1. Open the BunnyTools QR Code Generator and choose the WiFi option.
2. Enter your network name (SSID), password, and security type (usually WPA/WPA2).
3. Click Generate and download your QR code image.
4. Print it or show it on your phone screen for guests to scan.

On iPhone, just point the camera at the code and tap the popup. On Android, the camera or a QR scanner app does the same.

3 tips

First, test the code with your own phone before printing — a typo in the password means nobody connects. Second, print it large enough to scan from a meter away, and keep a digital copy too. Third, only share it with people you trust: anyone who scans it gets full access to your network, so change your password if the code ends up somewhere public.

The QR code is generated entirely in your browser — your WiFi password is never sent anywhere.
