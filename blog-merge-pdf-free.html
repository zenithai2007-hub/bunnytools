📄 How to Merge PDF Files Online Free

Combine your CV, degrees and certificates into one clean PDF — in under a minute.

Applying for a job and the portal asks for "one PDF" containing your CV, degrees, and certificates? Merging them one by one in a desktop app is slow, and many free websites add watermarks or force you to sign up. Here is how to merge PDF files online free in under a minute.

Why merge PDFs?

Recruiters prefer a single document — one file with your CV first, followed by degrees and experience letters, is easier to download, forward, and print than five separate attachments. Many government and university portals also accept only one PDF upload.

Merge PDFs in 4 steps

1. Open the BunnyTools PDF Merge tool in your browser — no account needed.
2. Upload your PDF files in the order you want them to appear (CV first).
3. Rearrange if needed, then click Merge.
4. Download your single combined PDF — no watermark, ever.

4 tips for a professional result

First, keep file names clear before uploading, like "CV.pdf" and "Degree.pdf", so the order is obvious. Second, remove blank or duplicate pages first with the Split tool if your scans have extras. Third, check the page count of the final file — a CV plus two certificates should be exactly what you expect, no surprises. Fourth, keep the merged file easy to email: most inboxes reject attachments over 20–25MB.

Everything is processed on your device — your documents are never uploaded to a server. That matters when your files contain personal details like your ID number or address.
